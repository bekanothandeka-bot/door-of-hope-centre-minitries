About Us

Door Of Hope Centre Ministries

Our purpose is to win souls for Christ through the Gospel we preach. We believe that Jesus is the Eternal Hope and that lives are transformed through the power of God.


Under the leadership of Prophet Kenneth Moses, the ministry is committed to prayer, revival, healing, deliverance and bringing people closer to Christ.
